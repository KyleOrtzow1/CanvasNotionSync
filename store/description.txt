Canvas-Notion Assignment Sync automatically keeps your Notion database up to date with assignments from Canvas LMS. Once configured, the extension runs in the background and syncs your courses, due dates, grades, and submission statuses — so you can manage everything in Notion without manual copy-paste.

Key features:

- No Canvas token needed — sync runs on your existing Canvas login, so it works even at schools that block students from creating access tokens (you can still supply a token if you prefer)
- Guided three-step setup — the extension adds the columns sync needs to a database you already have, and every field saves as you type
- Automatic syncing every 30 minutes, or sync manually with one click
- Smart updates — compares every assignment field-by-field and only writes to Notion when something actually changed, cutting Notion API calls by 70-80%
- Auto-check on completion — when an assignment first reaches Graded, Submitted, or Pending Review, its checkbox is ticked for you; untick a row by hand and sync leaves it alone
- Status preservation — manually set statuses like "In Progress" in Notion won't be overwritten unless Canvas reports forward progress (e.g., a grade)
- Deletion detection — assignments removed from Canvas are automatically archived in Notion
- Sync logs — a timestamped, colour-coded history of every sync, so you can see exactly what was created, updated, or archived
- Encrypted credentials — your Notion access token (and Canvas token, if you use one) are stored with AES-GCM 256-bit encryption, on your computer only
- Built to stay within limits — rate limiting for both Canvas and Notion, automatic retries with backoff, and storage monitoring with cleanup
- Supports all Canvas instances (*.instructure.com and *.canvaslms.com)

Getting started:

1. Create a Notion integration named "Canvas Sync" at notion.so/my-integrations and copy its access token
2. Make a database in Notion and add that connection to it ("..." menu → Connections → Canvas Sync → Add to page)
3. Open the extension popup and follow the three setup steps — paste your access token, paste your database link, then open a Canvas tab and hit "Set Up Database"
4. That adds the columns sync needs and runs your first sync — switch to your Notion tab to watch the assignments land

After that it keeps itself up to date in the background, and "Sync Now" is there whenever you want it immediately.

Built for students and educators who use both Canvas and Notion to stay organized.
