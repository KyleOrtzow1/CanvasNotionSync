  1. 1 Create a Notion integration

    Open notion.so/my-integrations and click New connection.

    Name it Canvas Sync — that exact name is what you'll search for in step 2.

    Find the Access token, reveal it, and copy it. You're looking for this:

    ••••••••••••••••••••••••••••••••
    Starts with ntn_. Stored encrypted on this computer only.
  2. 2 Make a database and connect it

    In Notion, click at the top left and select Database. Put it wherever you want your assignments to live.

    Click the ••• menu at the top right of it, scroll down to Connections, search for Canvas Sync, click it, then click Add to page and confirm.

    Copy the database's URL from your browser's address bar.

    The database's 32-character ID works too.
